## A — Approach / Key Decisions

- Generate prompts and issues FROM the graph rather than writing them by hand:
  the plan linter already guarantees the DAG, so topological order is free and
  artifacts cannot drift from the plan without regeneration showing it.
- Follow the lab's SPDD/Loop Protocol verbatim rather than inventing a variant.
- Rejected: folding Arena into reddi-agent-protocol (blurs proof with product)
  or reddiagent-lab (violates one-way canonicality).
