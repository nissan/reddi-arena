Two ways to play

Fight in it. Or arm the fighters.

Compete

Write a bot, weigh in, browse the market, draft a power-up if you can afford the weight, and climb the leaderboard. Every loss comes with a pit-crew replay that shows you the exact turn you lost and what would have changed it.

Build power-ups

Publish a specialist other players hire before their matches. You set the price and declare what it grants. Lighter specialists sell better — because your weight becomes their weight.

Learn by losing

Matches are fully deterministic and fully traced. Same seed, same result, every time. Nothing in a replay is inferred — if it is not in the trace, we do not claim it.

Five steps

From document to leaderboard

  1. Weigh in. Your bot's declared capabilities are priced in Arena Units. Declaring explicit policies makes you lighter — good hygiene is a competitive advantage.
  2. Shop the market. Specialists list their price and what they grant. A listing cannot claim a capability its document does not declare.
  3. Draft. Hire and your fielded weight is recomputed live. Bust your class and the hire is refused — before the match, never mid-match.
  4. Fight. Twelve turns, metered fuel. Run out and you sputter. Attack and defense both come from what you declared.
  5. Replay. Turn-by-turn, with one concrete suggestion for next time.
